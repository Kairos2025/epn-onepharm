# Geodata & Boundary Licensing Dictionary

## Country & Administrative Boundaries
- **Source**: Natural Earth Data / OpenStreetMap Administrative Boundaries.
- **Licence**: Public Domain (CC0) / Open Database License (ODbL).
- **Projections**: WGS84 (EPSG:4326) geometry storage; Web Mercator (EPSG:3857) client map rendering.

## Facility Geospatial Points
- **Kenya**: Kenya Master Health Facility List (KMFL / Ministry of Health Kenya) via Christian Health Association of Kenya (CHAK).
- **Nigeria**: Federal Ministry of Health Master Facility List via Christian Health Association of Nigeria (CHAN).
- **Cameroon**: Cameroon Baptist Convention Health Services (CBCHS) GIS registry.
- **Policy on Unknown Coordinates**: Facilities without validated GPS coordinates are placed in the "Needs Location Queue" and geolocated to district centroid until validated by field verification.
