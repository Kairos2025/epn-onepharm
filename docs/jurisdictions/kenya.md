# Jurisdiction Brief: Republic of Kenya (`KE`)

## UHC Mandate Alignment
- **Primary Commitment**: Quality Improvement and Financial Protection under Kenya's Universal Health Coverage (UHC) roadmap and Essential Medicines List governance.
- **Anchor Network Partners**: CHAK (Christian Health Association of Kenya) and MEDS (Mission for Essential Drugs and Supplies).

## Regulatory & Data Residency Requirements
- **Statutory Framework**: Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) registration rules.
- **Ministry Interoperability**: Kenya Health Information System (KHIS / DHIS2) inbound synchronization requires a signed technical data-sharing authorization (`khis_ke`).
- **Data Residency Rule**: Facility-level stock and dispensing records stay within the `KE` jurisdiction partition. Only de-identified, MOU-authorized aggregate indicators transmit to regional EPN surveillance views.
