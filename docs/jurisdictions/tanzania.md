# Jurisdiction Brief: United Republic of Tanzania (`TZ`)

## UHC Mandate Alignment
- **Primary Commitment**: Quality Improvement and Financial Protection through Drug Revolving Funds (DRFs) and functional Drug & Therapeutics Committees (DTCs).
- **Anchor Network Partner**: CSSC (Christian Social Services Commission).

## Regulatory & Data Residency Requirements
- **Statutory Framework**: Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act (2022) and Ministry of Health DHIS2 / eLMIS governance protocols.
- **Data Residency Rule**: Raw facility submissions remain partitioned under `TZ` residency controls; only consented aggregate indicators cross into EPN regional analytics.
