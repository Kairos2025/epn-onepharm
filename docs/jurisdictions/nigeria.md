# Jurisdiction Brief: Federal Republic of Nigeria (`NG`)

## UHC Mandate Alignment
- **Primary Commitment**: Coverage Extension and Quality Improvement across rural faith-based health facilities supporting malaria, maternal, and essential medicine supply chains.
- **Anchor Network Partners**: CHAN (Christian Health Association of Nigeria) and CHAN Medi-Pharm.

## Regulatory & Data Residency Requirements
- **Statutory Framework**: Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023) / NDPR and Federal Ministry of Health (FMOH) Health Information Policy.
- **Data Residency Rule**: Facility-level records remain isolated in the `NG` jurisdiction partition under institutional DPA governance before aggregate regional reporting.
